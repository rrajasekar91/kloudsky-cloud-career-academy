Enterprise Cloud Architect

Design Cloud Solutions. Think Like an Architect.

Learn how to translate business and technical requirements into secure, scalable, resilient and governed cloud architectures.

Architecture

Design cloud platforms, application architectures, networking, identity and security solutions.

Governance

Business Alignment

What You Will Design

  • Identity and security architecture

  • High availability and disaster recovery
